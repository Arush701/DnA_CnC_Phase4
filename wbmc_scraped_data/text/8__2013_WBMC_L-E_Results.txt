2013 WBMC Results
Leinfelden-Echterdingen, Germany
Bart & Kultur Club "Belle Moustache" eV.
The 2013 World Beard and Moustache Championships were held on November 2nd, 2013 in the Filderhalle in Leinfelden-Echterdingen, Germany, with 331 participants from 24 different nations, hosted by Bart & Kultur Club "Belle Moustache" eV.
