2025 WBMC Results
Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania
 Mad Viking Beard Club Pennsylvania
The 2025 World Beard and Moustache Championships were hosted by Mad Viking Beard Club Pennsylvania, and took place July 3rd, 4th, and 5th at Heinz Hall in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The Top 3 of each category are below with points awarded. For a full list of rankings in each category visit wbmc2025.com/results.
