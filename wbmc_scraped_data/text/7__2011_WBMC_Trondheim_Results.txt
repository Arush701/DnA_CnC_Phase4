2011 WBMC Results
Trondheim, Norway
Den Norske Mustach-club av -91
The 2011 World Beard and Moustache Championships were held May 15, 2011, hosted by Den Norske Mustach-club av -91 (The Norwegian Moustach-club of -91) at the Prinsen Hotel, located in the heart of the historic city of Trondheim, Norway. The Top 3 places for each category are listed below.
